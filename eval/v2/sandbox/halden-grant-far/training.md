# Training and compliance

Good Clinical Practice refresher training is due every two years. As of September 30, 315 of 338
staff who need it were current. The remaining 23 are scheduled for sessions in October and
November.

The new data-privacy module launched in July. Completion is at 88%, and managers receive a weekly
list of people who have not started. The quality team ran 4 internal audits in the quarter, on
sample handling, document control, supplier qualification and computer system validation, with no
critical findings.

Next quarter the team will pilot short video modules for site staff, who often cannot attend live
sessions because of patient visits.
