# Community and outreach

Halden staff volunteered 410 hours in Q3, most of it at the city science fair and the hospital
reading programme. The company's summer internship hosted 12 students from two local colleges;
three of them have applied for the graduate scheme.

The patient advisory panel met twice. Its members asked for plainer language in consent forms, and
the clinical team has rewritten the two longest forms, cutting them from fourteen pages to nine. The panel
will review the new versions in November.

The annual charity run raised $18,300 for the regional children's hospital, slightly more than last
year.
