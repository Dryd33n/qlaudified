# Driver survey results

The annual driver survey closed on September 15 with 318 responses, a 72% response rate. Drivers
rated route planning 3.8 out of 5, up from 3.4 last year, after the new dispatch app replaced paper
manifests.

The most common complaint was waiting time at customer docks. The operations team has asked the
five busiest customers to share their dock schedules a day in advance. Rest-area access on the
northern routes also came up often; two new partner truck stops were added in August.

Results by depot will be shared with depot managers next week, and each depot will pick two
improvements to work on before the spring survey.
