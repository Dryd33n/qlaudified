# Facilities: Riverside campus

The new cold-storage room in Building 2 was commissioned on August 28. It adds 36 ultra-low
freezer slots, which lets the biobank retire the last of its rented units at the Eastgate site.
Rental savings are about $4,600 a month.

Energy use across the campus fell 6% year on year, mostly from the lighting retrofit in Buildings
1 and 3. The car park resurfacing finished on time, and the cycle shelter now has 60 spaces after
staff asked for more.

The landlord has proposed repainting the Building 1 exterior next spring. Facilities is collecting
quotes and will bring a recommendation to the operations meeting in January.
