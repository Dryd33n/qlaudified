# Information security update

Phishing simulation click rates fell to 4.1% in Q3 from 9.7% at the start of the year. All office
staff completed the new security awareness module; warehouse staff will follow in October using
shorter tablet-based lessons.

The firewall replacement at the Leeds hub finished on August 19. Two minor vulnerabilities found in
the customer portal's penetration test were fixed within the agreed 30 days. Multi-factor sign-in
now covers 96% of user accounts; the rest are shared scanner accounts that the team is replacing
with individual logins.
