# Waste and recycling

Household recycling reached 47% of collected waste in Q3, the highest rate since the food-waste
service started. Missed bin collections averaged 31 a week, down from 52 in Q2, after two new
routes were added.

The garden-waste subscription has 18,900 households signed up. The recycling centre on Mill Lane
will change its winter opening hours from November 1. Fly-tipping reports rose slightly to 212 in
the quarter; enforcement officers issued 37 fixed penalty notices.
