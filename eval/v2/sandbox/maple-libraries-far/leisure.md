# Leisure centres

Swimming lessons at the three leisure centres had 2,450 children enrolled in the autumn term.
Gym memberships stand at 6,300, roughly level with last year. The sports hall roof at Eastfield was
repaired in August after leaks during the spring storms.

The operator's annual report shows a small operating surplus of $96,000. A consultation on new
opening hours for the Westbridge pool runs until November 15.
