# People update, Q3

Halden ended the quarter with 23 open roles, 9 of them in clinical operations. Time to hire
averaged 41 days, a little longer than the 37 days in Q2, mainly because of two senior
biostatistics searches.

Voluntary attrition over the last twelve months was 8.5%. Exit interviews most often mention
commute times to the Riverside campus, so the hybrid-work pilot was extended to the analytics team
in August. Thirty-one people took part in the mentoring programme this quarter, and the internal
mobility rate rose to 14% of filled roles.

The annual engagement survey opens on November 4. Last year's response rate was 78%, and the
people team hopes to beat it with a shorter questionnaire.
