# Brightwater Software: quarterly operating report

## Customers

Brightwater ended the quarter with 6,840 paying customers, including 412 on enterprise contracts.
The company added 530 new customers in the quarter, most of them through the self-serve sign-up
flow. Average contract value for new enterprise deals was $88,000 a year.

Expansion revenue, from existing customers buying more seats or add-ons, was $3.6M in the quarter.
The largest expansion came from a logistics customer that rolled the product out to 2,100 more
users.

## Product usage

Weekly active users reached 386,000 at the end of September. The share of customers using the API
rose to 44%. Mobile app sessions grew 21% year on year after the redesigned mobile editor shipped in
June. The most used new feature was shared templates, adopted by 1,950 customers within six weeks.

## Go-to-market

Sales and marketing spend was $11.4M. The sales team closed 63 enterprise deals, with an average
sales cycle of 74 days. Marketing-sourced pipeline was $27.5M. Website traffic was 1.15 million
visits a month on average, with organic search bringing in just over half.

## Finance

Revenue recognised in the quarter was $12.7M. Gross margin was 79.6%. Operating loss narrowed to
$2.05M from $3.8M a year earlier. Cash at the end of the quarter was $96.3M, with no debt. Free cash
flow was negative $1.1M, an improvement on the previous quarter.

## Infrastructure

Cloud hosting costs were $1.74M, or 13.7% of revenue, after the move to reserved capacity in the
main region. The platform team migrated the search cluster to a new version with no customer-facing
downtime. Average API response time was 182 milliseconds.

## People

Employee count was 497 at the end of the quarter. Engagement survey results showed 81% favourable
responses, and voluntary attrition over the last twelve months was 9.3%.

## Outlook

Management will present next year's operating plan to the board in December.
