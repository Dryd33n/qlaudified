# Procurement: vendor review

Procurement reviewed 27 active vendor contracts in Q3. Five were renegotiated, saving about
$62,000 a year in total, mostly on courier and lab-consumables contracts. Two vendors were
dropped after repeated late deliveries.

The preferred-supplier list now has 44 entries. Average invoice processing time fell to six days
from nine after the new approval workflow went live. Procurement will run a tender for the clinical
courier contract in December, since the current agreement ends in March.

Teams are reminded that purchases above $10,000 need two quotes.
