# Parks and green spaces

The parks team planted 640 trees in the spring season, mostly street trees in the northern wards.
Survival after the dry summer was checked in September: 588 trees are healthy and the rest will be
replaced in November at the contractor's cost.

The new skate park in Hillside Park opened on July 8 and has been busy every weekend. Litter
collections in parks were increased from three to five days a week during the summer holidays.
Volunteers from 14 friends-of-parks groups gave around 2,100 hours of work over the season.
