# Maple Street Council: quarterly performance report

## Finances

The council's revenue budget for the year is $186M. Spending to the end of September was $91.8M,
close to the profile. Council tax collection reached 57.6% of the year's amount by the end of the
quarter, slightly ahead of last year. Business rates collection was 58.9%.

The capital programme spent $14.7M in the half year, mainly on schools, the leisure centre roof and
the town-centre public realm scheme. Interest paid on borrowing was $1.06M in the quarter.

## Adult and children's services

Adult social care supported 2,315 people in the community and 640 in residential care at the end of
September. The average time from referral to assessment was 19 days. Children's services had 214
children in care, and 92% of them had an up-to-date care plan. Caseloads for social workers
averaged 17 children per worker.

## Environment

The council's own carbon emissions fell 11% compared with the baseline year, helped by the LED
street-lighting programme and the switch of 23 pool cars to electric. Air quality at the two
monitoring stations on the ring road stayed below the legal limit for the year so far.

## Housing

There were 1,180 households on the housing register at the end of the quarter, and 86 households
in temporary accommodation. The council let 147 social homes in the quarter. Average re-let time
for empty council homes was 31 days, against a target of 28 days.

## Customer and corporate

Complaints received in the quarter numbered 296, and 89% were answered within the 10-working-day
standard. Freedom-of-information requests numbered 278, with 93% answered on time. Staff sickness
averaged 4.6 days per full-time employee over the last six months.

## Community safety

Recorded anti-social behaviour incidents fell to 702 from 811 in the same quarter last year. The
community safety partnership ran 15 joint patrols in the town centre during the summer evenings,
and the CCTV control room supported 129 police incidents.

## Looking ahead

The medium-term financial plan will be refreshed in November, and the budget consultation opens in
early December.
