# Study A059

Title: Peer support groups and loneliness scores: a randomised trial
Intervention: Peer support groups
Outcome: loneliness scores
Participants: 742

Methods: Adults were randomised to peer support groups or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: In the peer support groups group, loneliness scores possibly fell by an estimated 15%.

Conclusion: These results suggest peer support groups may be worth further study.
