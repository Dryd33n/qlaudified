# Study A051

Title: Text-message reminders and missed appointments: a randomised trial
Intervention: Text-message reminders
Outcome: missed appointments
Participants: 310

Methods: Adults were randomised to text-message reminders or usual care and followed for 8 weeks.

Results: Text-message reminders might reduce missed appointments by up to 37%, the authors suggest.

Conclusion: These results suggest text-message reminders may be worth further study.
