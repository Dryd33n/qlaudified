# Study A039

Title: Pharmacist medication review and adverse drug events: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pharmacist medication review
Outcome: adverse drug events
Participants: 661

Methods: Adults were randomised to pharmacist medication review or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Pharmacist medication review may have reduced adverse drug events by about 37% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: These results suggest pharmacist medication review may be worth further study.
