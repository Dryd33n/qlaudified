# Study A092

Title: Text-message reminders and missed appointments: a randomised trial
Intervention: Text-message reminders
Outcome: missed appointments
Participants: 609

Methods: Adults were randomised to text-message reminders or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Text-message reminders may have reduced missed appointments by about 35% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: These results suggest text-message reminders may be worth further study.
