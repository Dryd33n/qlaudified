# Study A008

Title: Strength training and frailty scores: a randomised trial
Intervention: Strength training
Outcome: frailty scores
Participants: 842

Methods: Adults were randomised to strength training or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Strength training may have reduced frailty scores by about 19% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: These results suggest strength training may be worth further study.
