# Study A019

Title: Hydration reminders and urinary tract infections: a randomised trial
Intervention: Hydration reminders
Outcome: urinary tract infections
Participants: 582

Methods: Adults were randomised to hydration reminders or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Hydration reminders appeared to reduce urinary tract infections by roughly 20% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest hydration reminders may be worth further study.
