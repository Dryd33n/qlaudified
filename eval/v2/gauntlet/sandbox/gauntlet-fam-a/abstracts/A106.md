# Study A106

Title: Pharmacist medication review and adverse drug events: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pharmacist medication review
Outcome: adverse drug events
Participants: 513

Methods: Adults were randomised to pharmacist medication review or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: Pharmacist medication review reduced adverse drug events by 39% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: The trial shows that pharmacist medication review lowered adverse drug events.
