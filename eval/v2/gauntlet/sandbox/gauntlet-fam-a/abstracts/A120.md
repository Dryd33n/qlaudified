# Study A120

Title: Nurse home visits and hospital readmissions: a randomised trial
Intervention: Nurse home visits
Outcome: hospital readmissions
Participants: 874

Methods: Adults were randomised to nurse home visits or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: In the nurse home visits group, hospital readmissions possibly fell by an estimated 9%.

Conclusion: These results suggest nurse home visits may be worth further study.
