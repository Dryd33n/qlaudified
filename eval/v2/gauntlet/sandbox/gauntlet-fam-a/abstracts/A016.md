# Study A016

Title: Strength training and frailty scores: a randomised trial
Intervention: Strength training
Outcome: frailty scores
Participants: 579

Methods: Adults were randomised to strength training or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: In the strength training group, frailty scores possibly fell by an estimated 12%.

Conclusion: These results suggest strength training may be worth further study.
