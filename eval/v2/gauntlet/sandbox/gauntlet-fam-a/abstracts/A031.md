# Study A031

Title: Acupuncture and chronic pain: a randomised trial
Intervention: Acupuncture
Outcome: chronic pain
Participants: 475

Methods: Adults were randomised to acupuncture or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Acupuncture might reduce chronic pain by up to 29%, the authors suggest.

Conclusion: These results suggest acupuncture may be worth further study.
