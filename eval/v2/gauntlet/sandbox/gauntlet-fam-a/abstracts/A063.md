# Study A063

Title: Sleep hygiene coaching and daytime sleepiness: a randomised trial
Intervention: Sleep hygiene coaching
Outcome: daytime sleepiness
Participants: 181

Methods: Adults were randomised to sleep hygiene coaching or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Sleep hygiene coaching reduced daytime sleepiness by 19% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: The trial shows that sleep hygiene coaching lowered daytime sleepiness.
