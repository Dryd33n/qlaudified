# Study A012

Title: Massage therapy and post-operative pain: a randomised trial
Intervention: Massage therapy
Outcome: post-operative pain
Participants: 66

Methods: Adults were randomised to massage therapy or usual care and followed for 8 weeks.

Results: Massage therapy might reduce post-operative pain by up to 34%, the authors suggest.

Conclusion: These results suggest massage therapy may be worth further study.
