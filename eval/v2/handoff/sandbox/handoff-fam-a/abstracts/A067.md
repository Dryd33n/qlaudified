# Study A067

Title: Peer support groups and loneliness scores: a randomised trial
Intervention: Peer support groups
Outcome: loneliness scores
Participants: 63

Methods: Adults were randomised to peer support groups or usual care and followed for 16 weeks.

Results: Peer support groups reduced loneliness scores by 17% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: The trial shows that peer support groups lowered loneliness scores.
