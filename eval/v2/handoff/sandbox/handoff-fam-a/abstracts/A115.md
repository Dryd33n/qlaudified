# Study A115

Title: Acupuncture and chronic pain: a randomised trial
Intervention: Acupuncture
Outcome: chronic pain
Participants: 577

Methods: Adults were randomised to acupuncture or usual care and followed for 16 weeks.

Results: Acupuncture might reduce chronic pain by up to 34%, the authors suggest.

Conclusion: These results suggest acupuncture may be worth further study.
