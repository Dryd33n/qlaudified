# Sustainability notes

Corran's electric van pilot in the city centre completed its sixth month. The 20 vans covered about
212,000 kilometres with no missed rounds caused by charging. Drivers asked for more cargo shelving,
which the leasing company will fit in November.

Solar panels on the Leeds hub roof produced 410 megawatt-hours over the summer, covering roughly a
third of the site's daytime electricity. The packaging team switched two customer accounts to
reusable crates, removing about 9 tonnes of cardboard a month.
