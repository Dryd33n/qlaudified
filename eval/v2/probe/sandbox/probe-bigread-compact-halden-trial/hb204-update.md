# HB-204 phase II trial: Q3 update

Enrolment reached 386 patients by September 30, out of a planned 520. The trial is running at 31
sites, after 4 new sites in Germany were activated in August.

Q3 trial spending is estimated at $2.7M, subject to the finance team's quarter-end review. Q2
spending was $2.3M.

The database lock is provisionally scheduled for June 12, 2027, depending on enrolment pace.
