# Engineering offsite

Eighty-four engineers met in Lisbon for two days in September. Sessions covered the platform
roadmap, on-call health and a hack day that produced 19 prototypes. Three of the prototypes are
being considered for the next planning cycle, including a command palette for the admin console.

Feedback scores averaged 4.5 out of 5. People asked for more time with the product managers next
time.
