# Revenue snapshot

Annual recurring revenue (ARR) at the end of Q2 was $48.2M, as reported in the audited half-year
accounts.

ARR at the end of Q3 is about $51.9M, based on unaudited figures that finance expects to restate
slightly after the quarter close.

Gross revenue churn was 6.1% over the last twelve months. Net revenue retention was 112%.
