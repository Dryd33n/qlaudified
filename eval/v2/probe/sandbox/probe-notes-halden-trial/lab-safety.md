# Lab safety review, September

The quarterly safety walk-through covered all four wet labs on the Riverside campus. Inspectors
logged 7 minor findings, down from eleven in June, and no reportable incidents. Two fume hoods in
Lab C failed their airflow test and were serviced within 48 hours; both now pass.

Training completion stands at 96% of lab staff. The remaining 9 people are on leave or joined in
the last two weeks and are booked for the October session. Eyewash stations were flushed weekly
throughout the quarter, and the spill kits in Labs A and D were restocked after the August audit.

The safety committee will meet on October 21 to review the chemical inventory system, which still
lists about 140 expired reagents that need disposal. Facilities has asked for the disposal vendor's
quote by the end of the month.
