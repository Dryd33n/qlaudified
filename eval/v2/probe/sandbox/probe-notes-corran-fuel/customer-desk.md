# Customer service desk

The service desk handled 14,800 contacts in Q3. First-contact resolution was 81%, and the average
email reply time was 3.2 hours. Chat was added to the customer portal in July and now takes about a
fifth of all contacts.

The desk lost two experienced agents to retirement this quarter. Their replacements finished
training in September. The team lead wants to add weekend chat coverage, which would need 1.5 more
staff, and will bring a proposal to the November budget round.
