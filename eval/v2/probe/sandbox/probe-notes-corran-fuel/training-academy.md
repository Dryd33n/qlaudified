# Corran Academy

The driver academy graduated 46 new drivers in Q3, the largest cohort so far. The pass rate on the
first attempt at the practical test was 89%. Forklift certification courses ran at all three hubs,
with 120 warehouse staff certified or recertified.

Simulator time was increased from 6 to 8 hours per trainee after instructors noticed that new
drivers struggled with reversing into narrow docks. The academy's next intake starts on November 3.
