# Fleet maintenance

Planned maintenance compliance reached 97% in Q3. Tyre spend was $286,000, about the same as Q2.
The workshop trialled a new brake-pad supplier on 40 trucks; wear so far is similar to the old
supplier at a lower price.

Two older rigid trucks were retired after failing their annual inspection. The workshop's parts
inventory was counted in September and matched the system within 0.4%.
