# Corran Logistics: quarterly operating report

## Volumes

Corran moved 1.42 million pallets in the quarter across its three hubs, about level with the same
quarter last year. Parcel volumes from e-commerce customers grew faster: 3.9 million parcels, up
from 3.3 million. The Leeds hub handled 46% of all pallet movements; Bristol and Glasgow shared the
rest roughly evenly.

## Network

The linehaul network ran 1,960 trunk routes a week on average. Trailer fill improved to 78% from
74% after the planning team combined several low-volume routes between Bristol and South Wales.
Empty running, the share of kilometres driven without a load, fell to 17.2%.

Two subcontracted hauliers were replaced after repeated missed collection windows. Subcontractors
now carry 22% of trunk volume, down from 27% at the start of the year, as Corran's own fleet grew
by 18 tractor units.

## Customers

Corran signed four new contract customers in the quarter, the largest a home-furnishings retailer
expected to add around 9,000 pallets a month from spring. The retailer's volumes depend on its new
store openings, which have not all been confirmed. Customer satisfaction, measured in the
quarterly account survey, scored 8.1 out of 10. Two customers gave notice: one moving its
distribution in-house, the other consolidating with a rival after a takeover.

## Finance

Revenue for the quarter was $57.3M and operating profit was $4.25M, a margin of 7.4%. Staff costs
rose 6% after the annual pay award took effect in July. Capital spending was $2.9M, mostly on
tractor units and the Glasgow yard extension. Net debt at the end of the quarter stood at $21.6M.

## Safety

The lost-time injury rate was 0.62 per 100,000 hours worked, the lowest Corran has recorded. Two
reportable incidents occurred at the Bristol hub, both involving pallet trucks, and the hub has
since changed its pedestrian walkways. Vehicle collisions per million kilometres fell to 1.9.

## Outlook

For the next quarter, Corran expects volumes to rise seasonally, with the peak in late November and
early December. Temporary staffing plans cover about 240 extra warehouse shifts a week at the peak.
