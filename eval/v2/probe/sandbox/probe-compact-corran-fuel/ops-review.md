# Operations review, Q3

Fuel spend for Q3 came to $3.1M, according to the closed ledger. For Q4, fuel spend is forecast
at about $3.4M if diesel prices stay where they are.

On-time delivery was 93.5% in Q3. The average delivery took 26.4 hours from pickup, and early data
suggest Q4 could reach 24 hours once the Midlands cross-dock opens, though this has not been
modelled in detail.
