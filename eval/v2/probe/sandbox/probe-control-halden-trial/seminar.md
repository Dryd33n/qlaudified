# Science seminar series

The autumn seminar series opened on September 18 with a talk on organoid models, attended by 96
people in person and online. The next three talks cover assay automation, real-world evidence and
rare-disease registries.

Recordings of the spring series have been watched about 1,300 times on the internal portal. The
organisers would like more speakers from the clinical side and have asked managers for
nominations.

Coffee and pastries will continue to be served in the Building 1 atrium before each talk.
