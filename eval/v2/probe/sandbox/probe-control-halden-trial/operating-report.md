# Halden Biotech: operating report, third quarter

## Portfolio

Halden has five programmes in clinical development. HB-118, the oral anti-fibrotic, finished its
phase I dose-escalation study in July with no dose-limiting toxicities across six cohorts. The
safety review committee cleared the move to the expansion cohort, which will enrol up to 48 healthy
volunteers at two units in the Netherlands. HB-311, the antibody for a rare kidney disease, is in a
natural-history study that has collected baseline data on 73 patients so far; the team uses it to
choose endpoints for a future pivotal trial.

The discovery group nominated one new development candidate in August. Preclinical toxicology for
it is booked with a contract research organisation for the first half of next year, at a quoted
cost of $780,000.

## Partnerships

The co-development agreement with Ostra Pharma generated $1.35M in milestone income in the
quarter, triggered by the first patient dosed in the Japanese bridging study. Ostra pays for 60% of
HB-311 development costs under the agreement. Talks with two academic centres on a shared biobank
continued; neither has reached term-sheet stage.

## Operations

Average cycle time from protocol approval to first patient in fell to 142 days, from 171 days a
year earlier, mainly because site contracts now use a master template. Monitoring visits completed
on schedule: 94% across all active studies. The data management team closed 2,870 queries in the
quarter, and the median time to close a query was 9 days.

Clinical supply shipped 1,115 kits to sites without temperature excursions. One shipment to Poland
was held at customs for five days; the kits stayed within range and were released.

## Finance

Cash and short-term investments at the end of the quarter were $61.4M. Operating expenses for the
quarter were $13.9M, of which research and development made up $10.2M. The board approved a
revised capital plan that keeps the cash runway into the second half of 2028 at the current spend
rate. Interest income was $512,000, helped by higher deposit rates.

## People and culture

Headcount across the company was 212 at the end of the quarter. The leadership team welcomed a new
chief medical officer in September, who joins from a large vaccine company and will lead the
clinical development committee from October.

## Outlook

Management's priorities for the fourth quarter are the HB-118 expansion cohort, site activation in
Spain and Italy for the HB-204 programme, and closing the Ostra milestone invoice, which is due
within 45 days of the September dosing event.
