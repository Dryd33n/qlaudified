# Study A004

Title: Pelvic floor coaching and leakage episodes: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pelvic floor coaching
Outcome: leakage episodes
Participants: 185

Methods: Adults were randomised to pelvic floor coaching or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: In the pelvic floor coaching group, leakage episodes possibly fell by an estimated 18%.

Conclusion: These results suggest pelvic floor coaching may be worth further study.
