# Study A058

Title: Breathwork coaching and panic attacks: a randomised trial
Intervention: Breathwork coaching
Outcome: panic attacks
Participants: 197

Methods: Adults were randomised to breathwork coaching or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Breathwork coaching lowered panic attacks by 31% over the follow-up period.

Conclusion: The trial shows that breathwork coaching lowered panic attacks.
