# Study A105

Title: Dry needling and back pain: a randomised trial
Intervention: Dry needling
Outcome: back pain
Participants: 530

Methods: Adults were randomised to dry needling or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Dry needling lowered back pain by 37% over the follow-up period.

Conclusion: The trial shows that dry needling lowered back pain.
