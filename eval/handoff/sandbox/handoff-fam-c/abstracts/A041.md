# Study A041

Title: Art therapy and restlessness: a randomised trial
Intervention: Art therapy
Outcome: restlessness
Participants: 696

Methods: Adults were randomised to art therapy or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Art therapy appeared to reduce restlessness by roughly 22% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest art therapy may be worth further study.
