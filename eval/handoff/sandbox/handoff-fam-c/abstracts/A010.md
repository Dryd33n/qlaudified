# Study A010

Title: Deprescribing clinics and medication errors: a randomised trial
Intervention: Deprescribing clinics
Outcome: medication errors
Participants: 772

Methods: Adults were randomised to deprescribing clinics or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: In the deprescribing clinics group, medication errors fell by 41%.

Conclusion: The trial shows that deprescribing clinics lowered medication errors.
