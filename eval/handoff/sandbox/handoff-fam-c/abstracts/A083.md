# Study A083

Title: Dawn simulators and winter blues scores: a randomised trial
Intervention: Dawn simulators
Outcome: winter blues scores
Participants: 155

Methods: Adults were randomised to dawn simulators or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Dawn simulators lowered winter blues scores by 39% over the follow-up period.

Conclusion: The trial shows that dawn simulators lowered winter blues scores.
