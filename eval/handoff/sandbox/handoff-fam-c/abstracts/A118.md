# Study A118

Title: Cataract surgery and memory lapses: a randomised trial
Intervention: Cataract surgery
Outcome: memory lapses
Participants: 168

Methods: Adults were randomised to cataract surgery or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: In the cataract surgery group, memory lapses possibly fell by an estimated 36%.

Conclusion: These results suggest cataract surgery may be worth further study.
