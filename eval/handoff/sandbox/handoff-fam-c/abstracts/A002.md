# Study A002

Title: Pelvic floor coaching and leakage episodes: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pelvic floor coaching
Outcome: leakage episodes
Participants: 788

Methods: Adults were randomised to pelvic floor coaching or usual care and followed for 16 weeks.

Results: Pelvic floor coaching appeared to reduce leakage episodes by roughly 25% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest pelvic floor coaching may be worth further study.
