# Study A023

Title: Befriending calls and isolation scores: a randomised trial
Intervention: Befriending calls
Outcome: isolation scores
Participants: 54

Methods: Adults were randomised to befriending calls or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: Befriending calls lowered isolation scores by 15% over the follow-up period.

Conclusion: The trial shows that befriending calls lowered isolation scores.
