# Study A032

Title: Gardening programmes and low mood: a randomised trial
Intervention: Gardening programmes
Outcome: low mood
Participants: 795

Methods: Adults were randomised to gardening programmes or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Gardening programmes appeared to reduce low mood by roughly 15% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest gardening programmes may be worth further study.
