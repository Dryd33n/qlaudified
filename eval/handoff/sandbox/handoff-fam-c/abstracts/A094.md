# Study A094

Title: Cataract surgery and memory lapses: a randomised trial
Intervention: Cataract surgery
Outcome: memory lapses
Participants: 774

Methods: Adults were randomised to cataract surgery or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Cataract surgery may have reduced memory lapses by about 34% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: These results suggest cataract surgery may be worth further study.
