# Study A100

Title: Nap scheduling and afternoon drowsiness: a randomised trial
Intervention: Nap scheduling
Outcome: afternoon drowsiness
Participants: 448

Methods: Adults were randomised to nap scheduling or usual care and followed for 16 weeks.

Results: Nap scheduling may have reduced afternoon drowsiness by about 25% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: These results suggest nap scheduling may be worth further study.
