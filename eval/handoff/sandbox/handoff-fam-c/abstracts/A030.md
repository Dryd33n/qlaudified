# Study A030

Title: Pharmacy phone follow-up and emergency visits: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pharmacy phone follow-up
Outcome: emergency visits
Participants: 876

Methods: Adults were randomised to pharmacy phone follow-up or usual care and followed for 8 weeks.

Results: Pharmacy phone follow-up appeared to reduce emergency visits by roughly 13% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest pharmacy phone follow-up may be worth further study.
