# Study A022

Title: Pelvic floor coaching and leakage episodes: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pelvic floor coaching
Outcome: leakage episodes
Participants: 568

Methods: Adults were randomised to pelvic floor coaching or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Pelvic floor coaching might reduce leakage episodes by up to 25%, the authors suggest.

Conclusion: These results suggest pelvic floor coaching may be worth further study.
