# Study A069

Title: Sleep restriction therapy and night waking: a randomised trial
Intervention: Sleep restriction therapy
Outcome: night waking
Participants: 642

Methods: Adults were randomised to Sleep restriction therapy or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Sleep restriction therapy lowered night waking by 32% over the follow-up period.

Conclusion: The trial shows that sleep restriction therapy lowered night waking.
