# Study A063

Title: Deprescribing clinics and medication errors: a randomised trial
Intervention: Deprescribing clinics
Outcome: medication errors
Participants: 423

Methods: Adults were randomised to deprescribing clinics or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Deprescribing clinics reduced medication errors by 40% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: The trial shows that deprescribing clinics lowered medication errors.
