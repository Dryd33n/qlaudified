# Study A119

Title: Gardening programmes and low mood: a randomised trial
Intervention: Gardening programmes
Outcome: low mood
Participants: 339

Methods: Adults were randomised to gardening programmes or usual care and followed for 16 weeks.

Results: Gardening programmes may have reduced low mood by about 17% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: These results suggest gardening programmes may be worth further study.
