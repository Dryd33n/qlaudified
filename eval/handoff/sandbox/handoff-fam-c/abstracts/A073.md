# Study A073

Title: Pilates and morning stiffness: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pilates
Outcome: morning stiffness
Participants: 479

Methods: Adults were randomised to pilates or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: Pilates might reduce morning stiffness by up to 37%, the authors suggest.

Conclusion: These results suggest pilates may be worth further study.
