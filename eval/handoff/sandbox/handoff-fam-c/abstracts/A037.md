# Study A037

Title: Pelvic floor coaching and leakage episodes: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pelvic floor coaching
Outcome: leakage episodes
Participants: 262

Methods: Adults were randomised to pelvic floor coaching or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Pelvic floor coaching may have reduced leakage episodes by about 25% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: These results suggest pelvic floor coaching may be worth further study.
