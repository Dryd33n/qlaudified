# Study A104

Title: Pharmacy phone follow-up and emergency visits: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pharmacy phone follow-up
Outcome: emergency visits
Participants: 864

Methods: Adults were randomised to pharmacy phone follow-up or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: Pharmacy phone follow-up may have reduced emergency visits by about 14% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: These results suggest pharmacy phone follow-up may be worth further study.
