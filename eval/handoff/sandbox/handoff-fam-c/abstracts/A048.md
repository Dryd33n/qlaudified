# Study A048

Title: Pelvic floor coaching and leakage episodes: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pelvic floor coaching
Outcome: leakage episodes
Participants: 797

Methods: Adults were randomised to pelvic floor coaching or usual care and followed for 8 weeks.

Results: Pelvic floor coaching may have reduced leakage episodes by about 28% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: These results suggest pelvic floor coaching may be worth further study.
