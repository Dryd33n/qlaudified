# Study A014

Title: App notifications and late refills: a randomised trial
Intervention: App notifications
Outcome: late refills
Participants: 327

Methods: Adults were randomised to app notifications or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: App notifications appeared to reduce late refills by roughly 36% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest app notifications may be worth further study.
