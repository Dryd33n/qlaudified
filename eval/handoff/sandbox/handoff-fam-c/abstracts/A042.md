# Study A042

Title: Nap scheduling and afternoon drowsiness: a randomised trial
Intervention: Nap scheduling
Outcome: afternoon drowsiness
Participants: 688

Methods: Adults were randomised to nap scheduling or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: In the nap scheduling group, afternoon drowsiness possibly fell by an estimated 19%.

Conclusion: These results suggest nap scheduling may be worth further study.
