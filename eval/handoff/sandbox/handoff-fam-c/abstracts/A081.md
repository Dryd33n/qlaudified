# Study A081

Title: Pharmacy phone follow-up and emergency visits: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pharmacy phone follow-up
Outcome: emergency visits
Participants: 508

Methods: Adults were randomised to pharmacy phone follow-up or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: In the pharmacy phone follow-up group, emergency visits possibly fell by an estimated 13%.

Conclusion: These results suggest pharmacy phone follow-up may be worth further study.
