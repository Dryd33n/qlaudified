# Study A068

Title: Cataract surgery and memory lapses: a randomised trial
Intervention: Cataract surgery
Outcome: memory lapses
Participants: 56

Methods: Adults were randomised to cataract surgery or usual care and followed for 16 weeks.

Results: Cataract surgery might reduce memory lapses by up to 24%, the authors suggest.

Conclusion: These results suggest cataract surgery may be worth further study.
