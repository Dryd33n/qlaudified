# Study A077

Title: Pharmacy phone follow-up and emergency visits: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pharmacy phone follow-up
Outcome: emergency visits
Participants: 446

Methods: Adults were randomised to pharmacy phone follow-up or usual care and followed for 16 weeks.

Results: Pharmacy phone follow-up might reduce emergency visits by up to 12%, the authors suggest.

Conclusion: These results suggest pharmacy phone follow-up may be worth further study.
