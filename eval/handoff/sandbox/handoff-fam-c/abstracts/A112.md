# Study A112

Title: Pilates and morning stiffness: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pilates
Outcome: morning stiffness
Participants: 274

Methods: Adults were randomised to pilates or usual care and followed for 8 weeks.

Results: Pilates may have reduced morning stiffness by about 35% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: These results suggest pilates may be worth further study.
