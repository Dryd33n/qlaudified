# Study A047

Title: Water bottle prompts and kidney infections: a randomised trial
Intervention: Water bottle prompts
Outcome: kidney infections
Participants: 218

Methods: Adults were randomised to water bottle prompts or usual care and followed for 16 weeks.

Results: Water bottle prompts reduced kidney infections by 15% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: The trial shows that water bottle prompts lowered kidney infections.
