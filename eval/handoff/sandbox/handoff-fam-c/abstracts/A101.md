# Study A101

Title: Pilates and morning stiffness: a randomised trial
Intervention: Pilates
Outcome: morning stiffness
Participants: 520

Methods: Adults were randomised to pilates or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Pilates appeared to reduce morning stiffness by roughly 38% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest pilates may be worth further study.
