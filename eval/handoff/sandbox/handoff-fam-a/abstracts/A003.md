# Study A003

Title: Breathing exercises and breathlessness: a randomised trial
Intervention: Breathing exercises
Outcome: breathlessness
Participants: 508

Methods: Adults were randomised to breathing exercises or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Breathing exercises lowered breathlessness by 26% over the follow-up period.

Conclusion: The trial shows that breathing exercises lowered breathlessness.
