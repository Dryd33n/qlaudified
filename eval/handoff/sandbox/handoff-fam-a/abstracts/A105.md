# Study A105

Title: CBT for insomnia and insomnia severity: a randomised trial
Intervention: CBT for insomnia
Outcome: insomnia severity
Participants: 642

Methods: Adults were randomised to CBT for insomnia or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: In the CBT for insomnia group, insomnia severity possibly fell by an estimated 32%.

Conclusion: These results suggest CBT for insomnia may be worth further study.
