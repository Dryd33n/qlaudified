# Study A108

Title: Hydration reminders and urinary tract infections: a randomised trial
Intervention: Hydration reminders
Outcome: urinary tract infections
Participants: 218

Methods: Adults were randomised to hydration reminders or usual care and followed for 16 weeks.

Results: Hydration reminders might reduce urinary tract infections by up to 15%, the authors suggest.

Conclusion: These results suggest hydration reminders may be worth further study.
