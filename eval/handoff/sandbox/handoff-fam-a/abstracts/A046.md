# Study A046

Title: Massage therapy and post-operative pain: a randomised trial
Intervention: Massage therapy
Outcome: post-operative pain
Participants: 439

Methods: Adults were randomised to massage therapy or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Massage therapy appeared to reduce post-operative pain by roughly 28% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest massage therapy may be worth further study.
