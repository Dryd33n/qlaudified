# Study A039

Title: Strength training and frailty scores: a randomised trial
Intervention: Strength training
Outcome: frailty scores
Participants: 571

Methods: Adults were randomised to strength training or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: Strength training might reduce frailty scores by up to 16%, the authors suggest.

Conclusion: These results suggest strength training may be worth further study.
