# Study A043

Title: Strength training and frailty scores: a randomised trial
Intervention: Strength training
Outcome: frailty scores
Participants: 200

Methods: Adults were randomised to strength training or usual care and followed for 16 weeks.

Results: Strength training appeared to reduce frailty scores by roughly 16% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest strength training may be worth further study.
