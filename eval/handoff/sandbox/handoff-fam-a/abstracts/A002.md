# Study A002

Title: Hydration reminders and urinary tract infections: a randomised trial
Intervention: Hydration reminders
Outcome: urinary tract infections
Participants: 838

Methods: Adults were randomised to hydration reminders or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: In the hydration reminders group, urinary tract infections possibly fell by an estimated 17%.

Conclusion: These results suggest hydration reminders may be worth further study.
