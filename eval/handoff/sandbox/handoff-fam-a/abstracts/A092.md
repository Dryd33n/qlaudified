# Study A092

Title: Dietitian counselling and weight regain: a randomised trial
Intervention: Dietitian counselling
Outcome: weight regain
Participants: 852

Methods: Adults were randomised to dietitian counselling or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Dietitian counselling appeared to reduce weight regain by roughly 20% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest dietitian counselling may be worth further study.
