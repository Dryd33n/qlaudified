# Study A064

Title: Text-message reminders and missed appointments: a randomised trial
Intervention: Text-message reminders
Outcome: missed appointments
Participants: 327

Methods: Adults were randomised to text-message reminders or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Text-message reminders cut missed appointments by 36% relative to controls.

Conclusion: The trial shows that text-message reminders lowered missed appointments.
