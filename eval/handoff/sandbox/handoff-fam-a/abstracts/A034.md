# Study A034

Title: Acupuncture and chronic pain: a randomised trial
Intervention: Acupuncture
Outcome: chronic pain
Participants: 269

Methods: Adults were randomised to acupuncture or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Acupuncture may have reduced chronic pain by about 37% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: These results suggest acupuncture may be worth further study.
