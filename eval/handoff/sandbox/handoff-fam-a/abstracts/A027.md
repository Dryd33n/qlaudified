# Study A027

Title: Breathing exercises and breathlessness: a randomised trial
Intervention: Breathing exercises
Outcome: breathlessness
Participants: 423

Methods: Adults were randomised to breathing exercises or usual care and followed for 16 weeks.

Results: Breathing exercises lowered breathlessness by 19% over the follow-up period.

Conclusion: The trial shows that breathing exercises lowered breathlessness.
