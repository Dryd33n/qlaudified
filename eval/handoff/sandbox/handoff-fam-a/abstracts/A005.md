# Study A005

Title: CBT for insomnia and insomnia severity: a randomised trial
Intervention: CBT for insomnia
Outcome: insomnia severity
Participants: 360

Methods: Adults were randomised to CBT for insomnia or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: CBT for insomnia might reduce insomnia severity by up to 25%, the authors suggest.

Conclusion: These results suggest CBT for insomnia may be worth further study.
