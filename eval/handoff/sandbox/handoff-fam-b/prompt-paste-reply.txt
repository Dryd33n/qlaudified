# Intervention report

Source: `analysis/ranking.csv`, 20 interventions ranked by effect per $100 of cost per patient. Each effect is the unweighted mean of the six studies for that intervention. Rank is out of 20.

## Peer support groups

Peer support groups reduce loneliness scores by 18.17% (pooled across 6 studies, 2,016 participants).
Cost: $210 per patient.

Recommendation: Not recommended on cost-effectiveness. Rank 18 of 20 (8.65 per $100) at $210 per patient.

## Yoga

Yoga reduces fatigue by 36.33% (pooled across 6 studies, 3,066 participants).
Cost: $40 per patient.

Recommendation: Recommended. Rank 1 of 20 (90.83 per $100) at $40 per patient.

## Acupuncture

Acupuncture reduces chronic pain by 33.17% (pooled across 6 studies, 2,711 participants).
Cost: $60 per patient.

Recommendation: Recommended. Rank 2 of 20 (55.28 per $100) at $60 per patient.

## Text-message reminders

Text-message reminders reduce missed appointments by 34.17% (pooled across 6 studies, 2,596 participants).
Cost: $210 per patient.

Recommendation: Lower priority. Rank 12 of 20 (16.27 per $100) at $210 per patient.

## Nurse home visits

Nurse home visits reduce hospital readmissions by 13.5% (pooled across 6 studies, 3,956 participants).
Cost: $40 per patient.

Recommendation: Recommended. Rank 4 of 20 (33.75 per $100) at $40 per patient.

## Pharmacist medication review

Pharmacist medication review reduces adverse drug events by 38.17% (pooled across 6 studies, 3,070 participants).
Cost: $150 per patient.

Recommendation: Recommended. Rank 7 of 20 (25.44 per $100) at $150 per patient, with the largest mean effect of the six focus interventions.
