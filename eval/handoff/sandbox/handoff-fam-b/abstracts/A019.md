# Study A019

Title: Massage therapy and post-operative pain: a randomised trial
Intervention: Massage therapy
Outcome: post-operative pain
Participants: 164

Methods: Adults were randomised to massage therapy or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: Massage therapy lowered post-operative pain by 27% over the follow-up period.

Conclusion: The trial shows that massage therapy lowered post-operative pain.
