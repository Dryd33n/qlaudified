# Study A066

Title: Sleep hygiene coaching and daytime sleepiness: a randomised trial
Intervention: Sleep hygiene coaching
Outcome: daytime sleepiness
Participants: 99

Methods: Adults were randomised to sleep hygiene coaching or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Sleep hygiene coaching appeared to reduce daytime sleepiness by roughly 21% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest sleep hygiene coaching may be worth further study.
