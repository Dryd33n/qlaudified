# Study A014

Title: Text-message reminders and missed appointments: a randomised trial
Intervention: Text-message reminders
Outcome: missed appointments
Participants: 327

Methods: Adults were randomised to text-message reminders or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Text-message reminders appeared to reduce missed appointments by roughly 36% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest text-message reminders may be worth further study.
