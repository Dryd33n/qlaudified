# Study A002

Title: Bladder training and incontinence episodes: a randomised trial
Intervention: Bladder training
Outcome: incontinence episodes
Participants: 788

Methods: Adults were randomised to bladder training or usual care and followed for 16 weeks.

Results: Bladder training appeared to reduce incontinence episodes by roughly 25% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest bladder training may be worth further study.
