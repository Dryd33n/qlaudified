# Study A022

Title: Bladder training and incontinence episodes: a randomised trial
Intervention: Bladder training
Outcome: incontinence episodes
Participants: 568

Methods: Adults were randomised to bladder training or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Bladder training may have reduced incontinence episodes by about 25% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: These results suggest bladder training may be worth further study.
