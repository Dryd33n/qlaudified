# Study A111

Title: Walking groups and depressive symptoms: a randomised trial
Intervention: Walking groups
Outcome: depressive symptoms
Participants: 177

Methods: Adults were randomised to walking groups or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: Walking groups appeared to reduce depressive symptoms by roughly 20% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest walking groups may be worth further study.
