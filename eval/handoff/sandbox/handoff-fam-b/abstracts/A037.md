# Study A037

Title: Bladder training and incontinence episodes: a randomised trial
Intervention: Bladder training
Outcome: incontinence episodes
Participants: 262

Methods: Adults were randomised to bladder training or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: In the bladder training group, incontinence episodes possibly fell by an estimated 25%.

Conclusion: These results suggest bladder training may be worth further study.
