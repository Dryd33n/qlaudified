# Study A048

Title: Bladder training and incontinence episodes: a randomised trial
Intervention: Bladder training
Outcome: incontinence episodes
Participants: 797

Methods: Adults were randomised to bladder training or usual care and followed for 8 weeks.

Results: Bladder training may have reduced incontinence episodes by about 28% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: These results suggest bladder training may be worth further study.
