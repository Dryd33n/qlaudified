# Study A003

Title: Hydration reminders and urinary tract infections: a randomised trial
Intervention: Hydration reminders
Outcome: urinary tract infections
Participants: 717

Methods: Adults were randomised to hydration reminders or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Hydration reminders lowered urinary tract infections by 12% over the follow-up period.

Conclusion: The trial shows that hydration reminders lowered urinary tract infections.
