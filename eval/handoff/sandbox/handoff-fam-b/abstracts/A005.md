# Study A005

Title: Peer support groups and loneliness scores: a randomised trial
Intervention: Peer support groups
Outcome: loneliness scores
Participants: 897

Methods: Adults were randomised to peer support groups or usual care and followed for 8 weeks.

Results: In the peer support groups group, loneliness scores possibly fell by an estimated 23%.

Conclusion: These results suggest peer support groups may be worth further study.
