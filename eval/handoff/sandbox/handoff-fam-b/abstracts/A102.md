# Study A102

Title: Hearing aids and cognitive decline: a randomised trial
Intervention: Hearing aids
Outcome: cognitive decline
Participants: 845

Methods: Adults were randomised to hearing aids or usual care and followed for 12 weeks.

Results: Hearing aids appeared to reduce cognitive decline by roughly 28% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest hearing aids may be worth further study.
