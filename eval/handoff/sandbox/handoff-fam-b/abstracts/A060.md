# Study A060

Title: Dietitian counselling and weight regain: a randomised trial
Intervention: Dietitian counselling
Outcome: weight regain
Participants: 634

Methods: Adults were randomised to dietitian counselling or usual care and followed for 52 weeks.

Results: Dietitian counselling reduced weight regain by 18% compared with usual care.

Conclusion: The trial shows that dietitian counselling lowered weight regain.
