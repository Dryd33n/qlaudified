# Study A120

Title: Breathing exercises and breathlessness: a randomised trial
Intervention: Breathing exercises
Outcome: breathlessness
Participants: 202

Methods: Adults were randomised to breathing exercises or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: Breathing exercises appeared to reduce breathlessness by roughly 19% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest breathing exercises may be worth further study.
