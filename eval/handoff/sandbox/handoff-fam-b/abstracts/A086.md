# Study A086

Title: Mindfulness training and anxiety: a randomised trial
Intervention: Mindfulness training
Outcome: anxiety
Participants: 416

Methods: Adults were randomised to mindfulness training or usual care and followed for 16 weeks.

Results: Mindfulness training appeared to reduce anxiety by roughly 24% relative to controls, though the estimate is uncertain.

Conclusion: These results suggest mindfulness training may be worth further study.
