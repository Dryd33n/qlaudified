# Study A070

Title: Acupuncture and chronic pain: a randomised trial
Intervention: Acupuncture
Outcome: chronic pain
Participants: 412

Methods: Adults were randomised to acupuncture or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: Acupuncture lowered chronic pain by 33% over the follow-up period.

Conclusion: The trial shows that acupuncture lowered chronic pain.
