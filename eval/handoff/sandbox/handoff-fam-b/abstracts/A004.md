# Study A004

Title: Bladder training and incontinence episodes: a randomised trial
Intervention: Bladder training
Outcome: incontinence episodes
Participants: 185

Methods: Adults were randomised to bladder training or usual care and followed for 24 weeks.

Results: In the bladder training group, incontinence episodes possibly fell by an estimated 18%.

Conclusion: These results suggest bladder training may be worth further study.
